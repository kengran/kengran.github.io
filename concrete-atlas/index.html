Benchmark data and method

Mix inputs include five supplementary material fractions, water, aggregate and superplasticizer ratios, age, curing temperature and humidity, specimen volume and aspect ratio. The chemistry model adds binder-weighted CaO, SiO₂, Al₂O₃, Fe₂O₃, MgO and SO₃. Missing values use training-set medians.

Each split reserves 20% of source papers for testing. Both models use the same training and test rows, and the test set stays fixed as training size increases within an experiment. Points show mean root-mean-square error (RMSE) in MPa. Whiskers show one sample standard deviation across splits. A positive error reduction means adding chemistry improved the prediction.
